# Credits

Ice and Fire Fabric is an unofficial port of Ice and Fire to Fabric, for Minecraft 26.3. It is not made or
supported by the original mod's authors.

- **Alexthe666**: Ice and Fire, the original mod (LGPL-3.0): its code, creatures, structures, bestiary and texts.
  https://github.com/AlexModGuy/Ice_and_Fire
- **Raptorfarian**: the original mod's art (models and textures).
- **TheBv**: the original mod's port to newer Minecraft versions, which this port follows.
- **The original mod's contributors and translators**: its code over the years, and its bestiary and texts in other
  languages.
- **Escobar**: the Fabric port, and its new content: the exiled pixie and her memories, the citadel, Cryona's duel on
  Black Frost, and their music.
- The new voices and sound effects were made with ElevenLabs. The texts in the languages the original did not have
  were translated for this port.
